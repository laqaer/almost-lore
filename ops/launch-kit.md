# Launch kit (for the owner to post — the ops team cannot post)

Rules for every post: be the maker, say it's new, say an AI editorial team drafts and fact-checks
with a human publisher accountable, invite corrections, never claim traction you don't have, and
follow each community's self-promotion rules (read them first; some only allow it on certain days).
Space posts out over two weeks so each one gets your attention in the comments.

## 1. Show HN (Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am ET)

**Title:** Show HN: Almost Lore – a daily history game where the answer can be "almost"

**Text:**
I made a daily game about history's near-misses. You get five claims written as plain fact
("The United States bought Greenland from Denmark in 1946") and stamp each one HAPPENED, ALMOST
(it came documented-close but didn't happen) or LORE (a popular myth). Then the card flips to the
record with sources.

The interesting part for me was the middle verdict. True/false games are everywhere; "almost"
forces you to know *how close* something came — Truman really did offer Denmark $100M in gold.

How it's made: an AI editorial pipeline drafts claims with sources, a separate adversarial
fact-checker tries to refute each one, and anything where a fair expert could argue another verdict
gets cut. The rubric is public (/rules) and corrections are logged publicly (/corrections). I'd
genuinely like to hear where you think a verdict is wrong.

No account, no ads. Share results are spoiler-free, and "challenge a
friend" links keep your stamps sealed until they've played.

https://almostlore.com
