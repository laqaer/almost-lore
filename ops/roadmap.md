# Roadmap

Ordered by expected revenue per unit of effort. Items move up only when a metric earns it.
Anything that costs money or changes prices needs an `owner-action` issue first.

## Now (launch → week 6)
- **Keep the daily ritual alive.** ≥ 21 days of dockets scheduled at all times; weekly two-key review.
- **Halloween Edition** (live at launch) → **Thanksgiving Edition** by 1 Nov → **Christmas Edition**
  by 15 Nov. Search demand peaks at Halloween ~135k/mo, Thanksgiving ~110k, Christmas ~368k
  ("[holiday] trivia", KD 0–6, OpenSEO, Sept 2026). Each edition: free interactive round,
  printable sampler (email), paid printable pack.
- **"Thirteen Days" docket week, 16–28 Oct:** Cuban Missile Crisis anniversary, with the Arkhipov
  Case File as its anchor.
- **Marketplace mirrors:** Party Pack, Halloween Pack and Classroom Pack listed on Etsy and
  Teachers Pay Teachers by the owner (listing packs prepared in `ops/marketplace/`). New-domain SEO
  will be slow this season; marketplaces are where holiday buyers already search.
- **Case Files** on searched topics: Great Molasses Flood (~40.5k/mo, KD 19), Dancing Plague of
  1518 (~27.1k, KD 33), Emu War (~27.1k, KD 40), "Let them eat cake" (~40.5k, KD 14), Tulip mania
  (~12.1k), Stanislav Petrov (~9.9k), Vasili Arkhipov (~9.9k, KD 22), Defenestration of Prague
  (~8.1k, KD 11), Washington's teeth (~3.6k, KD 2), Was Napoleon short (~2.9k, KD 5).

## Next (weeks 6–16)
- **Classroom Vol. 2 and Vol. 3**, then the **180 Bell Ringers year bundle** (January, $29).
- **Archive Pass** ($3/mo or $24/yr): full archive play, a harder Sunday Grand Docket, monthly
  refill sheet for Party Pack owners. Needs Stripe subscriptions plus a signed entitlement cookie.
  Launch only when the archive has 60+ dockets and D7 ≥ 15%.
- **Upstash on** (free tier): "% of players who got this right" on reveal, with minimum samples.
- **Table / film mode** polish for reaction videos (the watermark carries the URL).
- **Office Edition push** in November for holiday parties ("christmas party games for work",
  "office christmas party ideas" — KD 0, CPC ~$6.5).

## Later bets (need evidence first)
- **Real Nights: murder-mystery parties set on real nights in history** ($39/kit, $199 office
  licence). Highest ticket in the concept tournament; launch only after the party-pack buyers list
  exceeds 500 and one kit passes a human playtest.
- **Year Party Kits** for milestone birthdays ("Born in 1966: Happened, Almost or Lore?"). Validate
  "[year] trivia" / "born in [year]" volumes first.
- **Physical deck.** Print-on-demand once ≥ 150 Party Packs sold; Kickstarter only once the deck
  waitlist ≥ 3,000 *and* print-on-demand sales prove demand.
- **Host Kit** ($9/mo): weekly 10-claim round + host script for pub quizzes, podcasts and streams.
- **Open claim dataset** (CC BY, versioned) for citations and backlinks.

## Explicitly not doing
Ads inside the game or classroom mode; fake social proof; user-generated claims before a
moderation plan exists; paid acquisition without the owner's approval.
