## 2. The Pig War: the border behind the joke

**Post copy**

A pig entered a garden. Troops and warships followed.

In 1859, a settler's shot exposed a border dispute on San Juan Island. The armies never fought a battle. The boundary remained unsettled until 1872.

What matters more here: the pig, or the treaty that left the border unclear?

**Image:** `/images/stories/san-juan-reservations-1860.jpg`.

**Credit:** Map of government reservations on San Juan Island, December 1860. U.S. National Archives. Public domain.

**Evidence:** [San Juan Island National Historical Park](https://www.nps.gov/sajh/learn/historyculture/the-pig-war.htm). Do not turn the familiar “only casualty” slogan into a claim about every death during the occupation.

Which failed invention would you most like to try?

**Post copy**

The burning ship is real. Its plunge over Niagara Falls is disputed.

In 1837, a British raid destroyed the American steamer Caroline. One man died on the dock. A later murder charge threatened peace again.

How much does a dramatic picture change the history we remember?

## 6. San Francisco: a documented experiment, an unresolved link

**Post copy**

In 1950, the military released bacteria off San Francisco. Residents were not told.

A hospital later reported a cluster of infections, including a death. The surviving evidence did not establish that the sprayed strain caused them.

What evidence would be needed to connect those events?

https://almostlore.com/stories/san-francisco-fog-1950?utm_source=brand_social&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=archive_overhaul&utm_content=san_francisco

**Image:** `/images/stories/san-francisco-fog-1950.jpg`.

**Credit:** USS Greenlet at Hunters Point, May 1950. U.S. Navy. Public domain. This contextual photograph does not show the operation vessel.

**Evidence:** [Steven Mahlen's published review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3194826/) explains why the hospital isolates could not be compared with the test strain. Keep that limitation in any shortened version.
