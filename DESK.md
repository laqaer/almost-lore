# Desk

Money is display ads on sourced longform. Nothing else is for sale.
Ads stay off until a real `ads.txt` seller ID exists. Do not invent one.
Do not add a newsletter, a membership, a shop, or an affiliate tag to get there faster.

## What a story is

A public-record near-miss someone already repeats in one sentence.
Original prose. The sentence goes in `hook`. The essay says where the sentence breaks.
If a count is disputed, name the dispute. Do not pick a favorite integer.
No scraped threads. No invented dialogue. No last words you do not have.
